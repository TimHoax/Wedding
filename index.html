Dress Code

Wedding Day: Summer Semi-Formal

As it will be a hot summer day, we want our guests to be comfortable and enjoy themselves. Full suits and blazers are not necessary. However, we do ask that you keep it semi-formal, so please avoid shorts, plakkies (flip-flops), jeans, and very short dresses.

As the venue is located on a mountain, the temperature may drop in the evening; therefore, we do recommend bringing something warm for the night.

Colours

Wear whatever makes you feel your best. That said, as it is a summer wedding, we would love to see some colour in our photos!

Pool

There is a swimming pool at the venue, so you are welcome to bring your swimming costume if you would like to make use of it during all the festivities or even for a New Year's swim!

Venue & Location

Noupoort Mountain Accommodation, Wedding and Conference Estate

📍 What3Words reference: ///blog.overcome.overthrew for precise navigation.

We are thrilled to celebrate with you at this beautiful mountain estate. The Ceremony, Reception and Pizza Night take place here.

Important: You will receive a gate PIN in order to enter the venue. As we are on top of a mountain there is limited reception, therefore please read the detailed directions below to avoid anyone getting lost!

Directions

About six kilometres after ascending the mountain pass to the venue, you will reach the first turn-off. It is next to a fenced-off, government-operated compound. Look out for the venue signage on a tree beside the compound. Turn left here and follow the road.

Approximately 4.6 km after this turn-off, you will come to a fork where a gravel road branches off to the right of the asphalt road. Take the right onto the gravel road, and keep an eye out for the venue signage.

After approximately 770 m along the gravel road, keep left and follow the main road. This will lead you directly to the venue.

Safe travels!

Wi-Fi & Cell Phone Reception

As the venue is tucked away in the mountains, cell phone reception is very limited and there is no Wi-Fi. We hope everyone will use it as an opportunity to disconnect for a little while and enjoy the beautiful surroundings and good company.

The venue manager, bride and groom will have access to Wi-Fi for organisational and communication purposes and emergencies however we have been specifically asked to not give this to anyone as it will cause the system to bottleneck.

A few things to consider before arriving:

  • Let pet sitters, house sitters, family or anyone who may need you know that you may be difficult to contact.
  • Download any music, movies, TV shows, YouTube videos, podcasts or children’s entertainment you might want during the stay.
  • If you need reception or supplies, you can drive down the mountain to Piketberg.

Frequently Asked Questions

Transport

Please be aware that Uber and other ride-hailing services are not available on the mountain, so please plan ahead and be conscious of drinking and driving. If you’re planning to drink and are staying off-site, please arrange alternative transport in advance. We want all our guests to be safe!

Elmarco Visagie | 061 897 8903 has been recommended as a local transport option by a guest house in Piketberg. We’ll also obtain additional recommendations from the venue and share these with you.

If you need transport from Cape Town or elsewhere to the venue, please let us know. We’ll happily help coordinate carpooling between guests or provide recommendations for alternative transport.

Kids

We’re so excited that our wedding will be family-friendly and can’t wait to have all the little ones celebrating with us!

We just ask that parents and guardians please keep an eye on their children throughout the day, particularly during the ceremony, speeches, special dances and other important moments, so that everyone can enjoy them. Once those are done, we absolutely cannot wait to see the kids taking over the dance floor!

As there is very limited reception and no guest Wi-Fi at the venue, please remember to pre-download any movies, TV shows, games, YouTube videos or other entertainment your children might want during the stay.

A little safety note: There is a swimming pool at the venue which is not covered or enclosed, so please make sure children are closely supervised around the pool at all times.
