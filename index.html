Venue & Location

Noupoort Mountain Accommodation, Wedding and Conference Estate

We are thrilled to celebrate with you at this beautiful mountain estate. The ceremony and reception will both take place here.

Directions

About six kilometres after ascending the mountain pass to the venue, you will reach the first turn-off. It is next to a fenced-off, government-operated compound. Look out for the venue signage on a tree beside the compound. Turn left here and follow the road.

Approximately 4.6 km after this turn-off, you will come to a fork where a gravel road branches off to the right of the asphalt road. Take the right onto the gravel road, and keep an eye out for the venue signage.

After approximately 770 m along the gravel road, keep left and follow the main road. This will lead you directly to the venue.

Safe travels!

Dress Code

Wedding Day: Summer Semi-Formal

As it will be a hot summer day, we want our guests to be comfortable and enjoy themselves. Full suits and blazers are not necessary. However, we do ask that you keep it semi-formal, so please avoid shorts, plakkies (flip-flops), jeans, and very short dresses.

As the venue is located on a mountain, the temperature may drop in the evening; therefore, we do recommend bringing something warm for the night.

Colours

Wear whatever makes you feel your best. That said, as it is a summer wedding, we would love to see some colour in our photos!

Pool

There is a swimming pool at the venue, so you are welcome to bring your swimming costume if you would like to make use of it during all the festivities or even for a New Year's swim!
